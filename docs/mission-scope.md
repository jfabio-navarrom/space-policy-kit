# Mission Scope: Vulpine-Earth

## Overview
* **Mission Name:** Vulpine-Earth (Commercial high-resolution Earth observation demonstration constellation).
* **Number of Satellites & Form Factor:** 2 nanosatellites in 12U CubeSat form factor.
* **Orbit:** Low Earth Orbit (LEO), Sun-Synchronous Orbit (SSO) at 530 km altitude.

## Frequency Bands
* **Telemetry, Tracking, and Command (TT&C):** S-band.
* **Payload Data Downlink:** High-speed X-band.

## Payload & Business Model
* **Payload:** Single high-resolution multispectral optical imaging payload (3 RGB bands + Near-Infrared NIR).
* **Business Model:** Wholesale catalog image sales and processed data infrastructure via APIs.
* **Target Customers:** Large aerospace integrators and geospatial service operators located within the European Union.

## Ground Segment & Infrastructure
* **Ground Segment Providers:** Global ground station network contracted through an external commercial Ground-Station-as-a-Service (GSaaS) provider.
* **Mission Operations Center (MOC):** Deployed within a public cloud environment.

## Out of Scope
* Internal design and manufacturing of the satellite bus and optical sensors.
* Physical hardware infrastructure of the ground stations (managed via external APIs).
* Intersatellite Optical Links (ISL) or real-time data relay constellations.
* Customers' internal IT systems and networks (operator responsibility ends at the API delivery boundary).
